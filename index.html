About

Koreana is an Android app that teaches Korean step by step, from the alphabet (Hangul) to advanced sentences and proverbs. It is available in Arabic, English and French.

  • Listening, reading, speaking (pronunciation practice) and vocabulary exercises
  • Spaced-repetition review: words come back when you are about to forget them
  • Grammar lessons and example sentences with audio and romanization
  • Personal account: your progress is saved and synced across your devices

Privacy policy

What we collect: your name, email address, learning progress (level, points, reviewed words) and any feedback you choose to send. Microphone audio is processed on your device by Android’s speech recognition only when you press the microphone button in the speaking exercise; we do not store recordings.

Where it is stored: on Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore). Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see them.

Payments: handled by Lemon Squeezy. We only receive confirmation that your payment succeeded and the plan you bought.

What we do not do: we do not sell your data and we show no ads.

Your rights: you can delete your account and data at any time in the app (More → Delete my account & data), or email us.

Children: the app is not directed to children under 13.
